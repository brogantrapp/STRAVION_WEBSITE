02 / ENGINEERINGSYSTEMS OVERVIEW

Built around the
whole flight.

A single airframe concept, considered from launch and cruise through transport, inspection, and service. These figures describe the modeled design configuration.

01 / TRANSPORT
FOLDING WING

Compact when it counts.

Hinged outer panels fold upward to bring the modeled span from 2.40 m down to roughly 1.43 m for storage and transport.

02 / PROPULSION
ELECTRIC PUSHER

Power, aft.

Modeled around a 6S electric system and a 15×8-inch folding pusher propeller.

03 / INTEGRATION
MODULAR ARCHITECTURE

Space for the mission kit.

Six internal bays provide approximately 17.4 L of modeled volume for avionics, batteries, wiring, and payload integration.
